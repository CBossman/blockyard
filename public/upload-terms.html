Upload terms

Version 1 · the rules for putting a game of your own on Blockyard

Blockyard lets you upload games you've made, and other people play them. Uploading means you agree to the rules below. Break them and your game comes down, and you may lose the right to upload.

Your game must be yours to share

  • Upload only what you made, or have the right to use (models, pictures, sounds, music and code included).
  • No real brands, characters, logos or trademarks you don't own: make up your own names and looks.
  • You keep what you made. By uploading, you let Blockyard host, run and show your game to players, and show it on the site (its name, cover and pictures), for as long as it's up.

What a game may not be

  • Nothing sexual, nothing hateful (attacking people for who they are), no gore for its own sake, nothing that harms or exploits children.
  • No harassing, threatening or targeting real people.
  • Nothing illegal, and nothing that helps someone do something illegal.
  • No scams: don't ask players for passwords, payment details or personal information, and don't pretend to be Blockyard or anyone else.

What a game may not do

  • No trying to break out of where games run, to reach other games, players' accounts, or Blockyard's servers, and no trying to get round the limits.
  • No mining, no attacking other sites, no using players' computers or Blockyard's for anything but your game.
  • No hiding what a game does from players or from Blockyard.

How games are shown

  • A new game is reachable by its link only. You can put it in the community directory yourself.
  • The home page is chosen: you can ask for it, and Blockyard decides.
  • Blockyard may take any game down, or off the directory or the home page, at any time, and may limit how many games, how much room or how many players an uploader has.
  • Games come with no promise that they'll stay up, keep their players' progress or keep working after an update to Blockyard.

Reports and takedowns

  • Anyone can report a game: use Report this game on its page. If something in a game is yours and you didn't agree to it being there, report it and say so.
  • Blockyard looks at reports, and may take a game down while it does.

Changes

These terms may change. When they do, you'll be asked to accept the new ones before you upload again.
